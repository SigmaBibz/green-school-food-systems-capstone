# Greening School Food Systems: A Low-Energy Digital Approach to Food Waste Tracking, Redistribution and Composting

**Author:** Bibiana Jumwa Muasya  
**Project type:** Green Digital Certificate Capstone  
**Status:** Proposed project blueprint; pilot outcomes have not yet been measured.

## Overview
This project explores how schools can improve food-waste management through a simple digital process while applying green digital design principles. It proposes three connected components:

1. **Smart Leftover Alerts** — scheduled Slack/SMS notifications to communicate surplus-food availability.
2. **Food Waste Tracker** — lightweight online forms for student-led waste logging.
3. **Compost Connect** — a static map connecting schools with local composting partners.

## The challenge
Surplus food may be discarded when schools lack simple ways to communicate availability, track waste and connect scraps with composting options. The digital tools used to support this process should also avoid unnecessary data transfers, repeated requests and resource-intensive features.

## Green design approach
- Schedule notifications rather than sending constant updates.
- Use lightweight forms and static pages.
- Compress any images that are necessary.
- Synchronize data periodically rather than continuously.
- Archive older records periodically.
- Minimize unnecessary data collection and page reloads.
- Verify hosting-provider environmental claims before making them.

## Proposed 60-day pilot
- **Days 1–7:** Obtain school/IT approval and configure alerts.
- **Days 8–14:** Train students and begin waste logging.
- **Days 15–30:** Connect with an initial composting partner and publish the map.
- **Days 31–60:** Monitor, evaluate and report findings.

## Measurement
Track food waste by weight, surplus-food alerts and collections, student participation, page weight, data transferred, request counts and synchronization frequency.

The proposed food-waste reduction target is 25%. Digital emissions reductions should only be reported after a defensible baseline and measurement method have been established. Proposed targets are not achieved results.
